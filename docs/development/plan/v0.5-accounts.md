# v0.5: accounts replace workspaces

The owner's words of 2026-11-11, designed in [software-design/](../../software-design/README.md) (the design
step is done): start with [accounts](../../software-design/app/accounts.md) and the decisions
[D-APP-27 to D-APP-29](../../software-design/decisions/accounts.md), D-UI-13, D-UI-14 and D-RULES-6.
[code-rules.md](../code-rules.md) is binding for every line and every subagent; a task report states *size
gate passed, no new lint suppression, coverage not lower*.

## What the owner asked for

1. The concept **workspace** goes; it is called **account**.
2. New batch no longer asks for a workspace first. Its read takes every shop's PDF and finds each order's
   account from the **shop name** on its label.
3. A shop name that no account lists is asked about: which account it belongs to, or a **New account**.
4. A batch's print may hold orders of several accounts.
5. Batch numbers are unified: one count per date, restarting each date.
6. Search covers all accounts by default; a filter narrows it to one shop or one account.
7. A Search result shows the **shop name** on the first row of its Order column.
8. The batch summary and the day summary can be made for one account or for all; summary PDFs get no
   **Print**, **Open** stays.
9. The daily order count and the other facts of one account move to a new page (the Accounts screen); the
   shops, rename and delete move there from Settings.
10. The accounts live under `accounts` in `packing-settings.toml`; one `session.toml` per date.
11. v0.4 data: **a simple Python script to migrate, completely separate from the program**
    ([migration](../../software-design/data/migration.md)); the program itself does not read v0.4 folders.
12. Always commit and push when done.

Q-APP-12 to Q-APP-17, Q-UI-9, Q-UI-10 and Q-RULES-3 are answered and recorded. **Q-APP-18** (the saved orders
of a renamed account) is open with its default A, which the work uses.

## Checks

- While working: `cargo test -p <crate>`. Before every commit: `bash tools/check.sh --coverage`.
- After any app development: `bash tools/make-portable.sh`. The handoff reports it, and says so if the build
  fails.
- Fixtures use made-up shops and accounts (*Toko Alfa*, *Toko Beta*, *Toko Gamma*) and made-up IDs
  (`580000000000000001`). The owner's shop names, buyers and IDs from `samples/`, `work/` or `tmp/` never

  only.
