# Open questions

Questions that need the owner's choice. Each explains the problem, gives options with an example of
what the owner would see, and marks one **Default**: what the app does until the owner decides, so
the work is not blocked.

A question is removed from its file once its answer is written into the other design files and
recorded in `decisions/`. New questions take the next free number of their scope; numbers are never
reused.

- [Open questions: labels](label.md) — `Q-LABEL-1` to `Q-LABEL-3`, `Q-LABEL-5`, `Q-LABEL-7` and `Q-LABEL-8`.
- [Open questions: app](app.md) — `Q-APP-1` to `Q-APP-3` and `Q-APP-8` to `Q-APP-11`.
- [Open questions: screens](ui.md) — `Q-UI-1` to `Q-UI-4`.
- [Open questions: the rules file](rules.md) — `Q-RULES-1` (several sets of categories), `Q-RULES-2` (renaming a grouping).
- [Open questions: sessions](sessions.md) — `Q-APP-4`, `Q-APP-5` and `Q-UI-5` to `Q-UI-7` (the day's sessions and the side panel).
- [Open questions: repeated orders and the page check](duplicates.md) — `Q-LABEL-9`, `Q-LABEL-10`, `Q-APP-6`, `Q-APP-7` and `Q-UI-8`.
